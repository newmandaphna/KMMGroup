How the portals work

Why there are three portals

Each practice is its own company with its own secure system, so your documents stay with the team that does your work. If you work with more than one practice, you will have a login for each, and the practices coordinate on our side.

I have a question but no account yet

Use Send a question above or message a department. It reaches the practice directly, and they reply by email with an invitation to the portal if you decide to work together.

I forgot which practice I use

Klein Muskat handles personal returns, trusts, and estates. Klein Mirsky handles bookkeeping, payroll, and business tax. Klein Real Estate handles property owners and investors. Your engagement letter and invoices carry the practice name.
