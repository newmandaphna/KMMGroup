How we work

Clients come to KMM because their tax picture has more than one moving part. Our structure is the answer: dedicated practices with a shared standard, so nothing falls between firms.

About the firm

  1. Specialists in each field

    Each practice does one thing and does it every day. Your return is prepared by people who work in that area full time.

  2. One relationship across practices

    When a business, a trust, and a building all belong to the same family, the practices coordinate internally. You manage one relationship.

  3. Planning ahead of the deadline

    Bookkeeping stays current and projections run before year-end, so filing season confirms a plan instead of discovering a problem.

  4. Reviewed with you before filing

    Returns and filings are checked against the source documents before anything is submitted, and you see the result before we file.
