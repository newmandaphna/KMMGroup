About KMM Tax Group

KMM Tax Group is three tax practices under one name: Klein Muskat, Klein Mirsky, and Klein Real Estate. Each is a separate company with its own staff and its own client portal. They share clients, and they share a standard for the work.

Most of our clients need more than one of the three. The owner of a company also has a personal return and, eventually, an estate. A family trust owns a rental building. A real estate partnership needs its books closed before anyone can talk about depreciation. When three unrelated firms each hold a piece, the pieces do not add up.

Here, one practice handles each piece: Klein Muskat the individual, trust, and estate returns; Klein Mirsky the business books and returns; Klein Real Estate the property entities. When a client uses more than one, the practices work from the same file.
