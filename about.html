About the firm

One group, specialized tax practices.

KMM Tax Group was built on a simple idea: the depth of a specialist and the breadth of a full-service firm should not be a trade-off. Specialized divisions, one standard of care.

Most tax problems do not respect tidy categories. A business owner has an estate plan. A family's trust holds rental property. A real estate partnership needs clean books before it needs a clever strategy. Handled by separate, disconnected firms, the gaps between those needs are where mistakes and missed opportunities live.

KMM Tax Group closes those gaps. Under one roof we run dedicated, specialized practices. Today that means Klein Muskat for individuals, trusts and estates; Klein Mirsky for business tax and bookkeeping; and Klein Real Estate for property and investors, with room to add more as the group grows. Each is led by people who work in that area every day, and they talk to each other.
