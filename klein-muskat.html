Personal tax, trusts, and estates

Klein Muskat serves individuals and families across the full arc of personal taxation, from the annual return to fiduciary filings and the transfer of wealth, with the discretion these matters call for.

Schedule a consultation Become a client Client login

Klein Muskat is the personal-tax practice of KMM Tax Group. The work ranges from straightforward individual returns to multi-state filings, fiduciary returns for trusts and estates, and the tax side of estate and succession planning.

Trustees and executors come to the practice for accountings that hold up to beneficiary and court scrutiny. Families come for continuity: the same people handling the parents' returns, the trust, and the next generation.

What Klein Muskat handles

01

Individual income tax (Form 1040)

Federal and multi-state returns for individuals and families, including complex and high-net-worth situations.

02

Trust and estate income tax (Form 1041)

Fiduciary income tax returns for trusts and estates, prepared with attention to beneficiary reporting and distribution timing.

03

Estate and gift tax

Preparation and planning support for estate and gift tax filings (Forms 706 and 709) and lifetime transfer strategy.

04

Fiduciary accounting

Accountings for trustees and executors that hold up to beneficiary and court scrutiny.

05

Estate and succession planning

Coordination with your attorney and advisors on the tax side of transferring wealth.

06

Tax projections and planning

Year-end projections, estimated payments, and planning around income events, sales, and retirement.
