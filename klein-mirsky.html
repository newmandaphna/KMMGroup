Klein Mirsky is a full-service CPA and bookkeeping firm built for small businesses. We keep your finances organized and current so you can focus on running the business — while the books, filings, and planning stay handled and ahead of schedule.

Licensed in New York and New Jersey, the practice pairs hands-on small-business accounting, bookkeeping, and payroll with experience across real estate, tax, estate, and trust planning — covering owner-operators from day-to-day books to long-term strategy.
