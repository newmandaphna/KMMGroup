Common questions

Before you reach out

What is the difference between the practices?

Each practice is a focused business. Klein Muskat handles personal income tax, trusts, and estates. Klein Mirsky handles business tax and year-round bookkeeping. Klein Real Estate handles the tax issues specific to property owners, investors, and real estate entities. Many clients work with more than one.

I am not sure which practice I need. Where do I start?

Choose General inquiry in the form and describe your situation. We route you to the right practice, and if your needs span more than one, we coordinate internally so you manage a single relationship.

How do I share documents securely?

Each practice uses a secure client portal for document exchange, e-signatures, and messaging. You can reach your portal any time from the Client Login menu at the top of the site. Please do not send tax documents through this form.

Do you work with clients year round or only at tax time?

Year round. Our work is built around planning ahead of deadlines, with projections, bookkeeping, and strategy, so filing season is a confirmation rather than a scramble.
