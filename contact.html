Common questions

What is the difference between the practices?

Each practice is a separate company with its own specialty. Klein Muskat works with individuals, trustees, and executors. Klein Mirsky works with businesses and their owners, including the owner's personal return. Klein Real Estate works with property owners, investors, and real estate entities. You deal with one practice, and the group coordinates behind it.

I am not sure which practice I need. Where do I start?

Choose General inquiry and describe the situation. Whichever practice fits will reply. If two are needed, both will be on the file, and you will still deal with one person.

How do I share documents securely?

Each practice uses a secure client portal for document exchange, e-signatures, and messaging. You can reach your portal any time from the Client Login menu at the top of the site. Please do not send tax documents through this form.

Do you work with clients year round or only at tax time?

Year round. Bookkeeping, projections, and estimated payments run between filing seasons, and the portal is open all year.
